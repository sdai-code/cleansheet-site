Privacy Policy — CleanSheet for Google Sheets™

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use Disclosure:
CleanSheet’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Minimal OAuth Permissions We Request

CleanSheet is engineered on a strict least-privilege architecture and requests only two narrow Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly — Allows CleanSheet to read and update cells only in the single active spreadsheet where you explicitly launch the CleanSheet add-on. CleanSheet has zero access to your Google Drive or any other spreadsheets in your account.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui — Allows CleanSheet to render its 300px sidebar interface inside Google Sheets.

2. How Your Spreadsheet Data Is Processed

  • 100% In-Browser Deterministic & Fuzzy Processing: When you run Remove Duplicates, Combine Duplicate Rows, Split Names, E.164 Phone Formatting, Email Normalization, URL Tracking Parameter Stripping, Change Case, or Whitespace/HTML Trimming, your spreadsheet cells are processed entirely inside your web browser and Google Apps Script. None of this data is transmitted to external servers.
  • Optional Micro-AI Batch Normalizer: Only when you explicitly click the optional Micro-AI Batch Normalizer button are the cells of your selected column transmitted over encrypted TLS to our stateless normalization endpoint to return standardized values. We never use customer spreadsheet data to train or fine-tune AI models.

3. Data Storage, Backups & Scenarios

  • 1-Click Undo Backups: Backup snapshots are stored as hidden tabs (_CleanSheet_Backup_*) directly inside your own active Google Sheet—never on external servers.
  • Saved Scenarios: Your saved Scenario presets are stored in Google Apps Script’s per-user PropertiesService.getUserProperties() container associated with your Google account.
  • Account & Quota Metadata: To enforce Free vs. Pro monthly Micro-AI row quotas, we store an anonymized user identifier (Session.getTemporaryActiveUserKey()), plan tier, and credit counter.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share Google user data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Payment processing for Pro plans is handled directly by Stripe or Lemon Squeezy; CleanSheet never stores your credit card details.

5. Data Deletion & Contact

You may delete all saved scenarios and hidden backup tabs directly from the CleanSheet sidebar at any time, or revoke CleanSheet’s access via your Google Account Permissions page. For privacy inquiries or deletion requests, contact sdai.code@gmail.com.
