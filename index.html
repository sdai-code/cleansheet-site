Frequently Asked Questions

Why does CleanSheet only ask for the spreadsheets.currentonly permission?
CleanSheet is architected so that it can only access the single Google Sheet where you actively open the sidebar. It never requests Google Drive access and cannot see any other files in your account.
Does my spreadsheet data leave my browser?
For all deterministic and fuzzy tools (Remove Duplicates, Combine Duplicate Rows, Split Names, E.164 Phone Formatting, Email & UTM Cleaning, Change Case, and Space/HTML Trimming), 100% of processing runs locally inside your browser iframe. Only if you explicitly click the optional Micro-AI Batch Normalizer button are the selected column cells sent over TLS to our stateless normalizer API.
How does 1-Click Undo work without Google Drive permissions?
Before applying changes, CleanSheet snapshots your active sheet into a hidden backup tab (_CleanSheet_Backup_...) inside the same spreadsheet. Clicking 1-Click Undo restores your exact values and formatting and removes the temporary backup tab.
